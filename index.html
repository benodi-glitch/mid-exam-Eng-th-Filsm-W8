Exam rules

  • The exam has 50 multiple-choice questions: Shadowing expressions (Q1–13), Collocations (Q14–25), Phrasal verbs (Q26–37) and Idioms (Q38–50).
  • Each question has one best answer. Unanswered questions score zero, and there is no penalty for guessing.
  • The 90-minute timer starts as soon as you press Start Exam. Refreshing the page does not reset it.
  • Copy, cut, paste, text selection, right-click and drag-and-drop are disabled.
  • If you leave this window or tab, you get one final warning. If you leave a second time, your exam is locked automatically and you can no longer change your answers.
  • Before you can press Finish & Submit, you must first press Save as PDF and save your PDF file. If you change an answer afterwards, save the PDF again. Then submit the PDF as your instructor tells you.
  • Work on your own. Do not use notes, other people, dictionaries, or AI tools.
